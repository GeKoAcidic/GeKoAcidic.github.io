Say That Again

Project Overview

Say That Again was built as an auditory perception and cognitive function training game that was created as part of the MQU PACE (Professional and Community Engagement) program, where the university pairs groups of third year game dev students with actual industry partners.

My group got partnered with Yiliyapinya Indigenous organisation to create a brain game to assist in the education of marginalised groups living in less privileged environments. We were given a relatively loose design brief and together came up with a design and a plan on how to execute it.

The purpose of the game was to have a customer order with a lot of background noise to make it hard to hear, and the player had to correctly fill out the order.

Whilst the game was built within a react application, a predominant amount of the work I did was outside of programming, as every member of the group were completing some variant of a programming degree, so we had enough developers on board. I was however, one of the only native English speakers on the team, I was the primary correspondent between the group and the client. Additionally, a majority of the voice acting in the game was done by me.
