Ransack!

Project Overview

Ransack! was developed as part of my Macquarie University Games Project B, normally you would continue developing the game you developed in Games Project A, however, since two out of four group members weren’t continuing our group disbanded and I instead joined the Ransack! team to help further develop their game in unity and finally publish it on itch.io.

During the term I was with the Ransack! team I took on a variety of roles such gameplay programmer, level designer and primary QA tester.

Noticeable contributions:

  • Moving platforms – To add more depth and movement to the environments.
  • Overworld / level select map functionality – To give players a central hub to swap between levels.
  • Tutorial level design and creation – First level players play, more utilitarian than most, gives players a basic run down of the game.
  • Secret Objectives functionality – Secret objectives added another dimension to the game that gave the players something else to strive for other than just completing the level
  • Primary play tester and bug finder/fixer – Unnatural skills for finding bugs in the game unfortunately left me with a lot of work fixing said bugs.
  • And many smaller tweaks, fixes and optimisations

Ransack! was also chosen to represent MQ Uni at both SXSW Sydney’s 2024 games expo and a handful of MQUs own exhibitions.

The game can be found here on both the Games@MQ and KitKat5100 Itch.io pages.
