Solo Project

Sea Shot

Project Overview

Sea Shot was the main solo project I undertook whilst at MQU, whilst I did finish the course with a functional VR 3D application, a major part of the project involved reading and researching into the fundamentals behind VR experiences, and what makes them so immersive. Additionally, I wanted to split my research partially between the VR aspect of the immersion, and also into researching what would need to be replicated in unity to create a particularly immersive scene.

This was very much a learning task for me as I was given the opportunity to mess around with lighting, sound engineering, terrain forming and entity AI creation. Alongside these fundamentals of game development, I spent an exceptional amount of time learning how to integrate VR into my program including not just the view port but using VR hand controls as well.

Unfortunately, there is no official release for this program yet.
